Instrument Booking

To ensure instrument availability and allow sufficient time for scheduling, please follow the booking procedure outlined below.

Booking Procedure

  1. Plan Ahead
    Please submit your booking request at least 2–3 weeks in advance.
  2. Submit a Booking Request
    Complete the booking form on our Google Site, specifying your preferred dates and the expected duration of use.

    Open the Booking Form
  3. Request Review
    Your booking request will be reviewed. You will receive a confirmation once your request has been approved.
  4. Instrument Handover
    Once approved, a date and time will be arranged for collecting the instrument.
  5. Instrument Return
    A return date will be agreed upon during the handover process. Please ensure that the instrument is returned on the scheduled date.
